Behind The Lens

Hello, I'm Nuwaisir Suhail Srishty.

Photography, for me, is more than capturing faces—it's about preserving emotions, stories, and fleeting moments that deserve to live forever.

Every photograph is created with patience, creativity, and a passion for authentic storytelling. Rooted in Motijheel, Dhaka, I approach every subject with an editorial lens, honoring light, shadow, and quiet human dignities.

01

Patience

Waiting for the exact second where expression meets raw emotion.

02

Authenticity

Zero artificial poses. Only true human connection captured softly.

The Photography Process

Discovery & Rhythm
